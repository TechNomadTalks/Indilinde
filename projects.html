The Green Net NPC

Projects, campaigns and collaborations.

Since launching on 29 February 2020, The Green Net NPC has run practical environmental projects across the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast — alone, with residents, and alongside partner organisations. Below is the timeline of what has been completed, what is in progress, and what is planned next.

Timeline

From launch to today.

Feb 2020 Completed

The Green Net NPC officially launched

Founded by Helen Dodge and a small group of South Coast residents. Registered as a not-for-profit company on 29 February 2020. Initial project areas announced: small-scale farming and permaculture, beach and river cleaning, recycling and repurposing, sustainable building materials, and opposition to new environmental hazards.

Mar–May 2020 Completed

Community food gardens — Louisiana & Tweni

Launched during lockdown to help families achieve food security without relying on food parcels. Distributed vegetable seedlings, composters, and weekly seeds to 18 groups of four households in Louisiana, Tweni, and surrounding townships. Some families started a crèche garden. The initiative was designed to continue after lockdown.

Sep 2021 Completed

International Coastal Clean-up Day — Port Shepstone

The Green Net organised a beach clean-up at Port Shepstone, joined by surfers from Genesis Waves of Hope. A windproof bin was erected, information about offshore mining was shared, and a petition was made available for signatures.

Dec 2021 Completed

NO to oil drilling — beach gatherings

Helen Dodge and The Green Net organised mass beach gatherings at 17+ South Coast locations — Sheppie, Tweni, Sea Park, Southport, Sunwich Port, Banana Beach, Umzumbe, Pumula, Hibberdene, Umkomaas, Ifafa, Mthwalume, Pennington, Scottburgh and others — to protest Shell’s planned seismic surveying on the Wild Coast.

Jun 2022 Completed

Shell seismic survey protests — Tweni Beach

The Green Net joined countrywide protests against Shell’s seismic study on Tweni Beach. Helen Dodge told Fever that the protests were iconic and were setting the scene for how ocean exploration should be addressed. The protests continued as the High Court heard arguments in Gqeberha.

Mar 2023 Completed

River Rangers Project conceptualised

The Green Net NPC conceptualised the River Rangers Project during National Water Week, designed to monitor and restore polluted rivers in the Ugu region through community cooperation, water testing, and low-cost remediation. Aims to empower communities to protect their drinking water sources.

Nov 2023 Completed

Power Up activation — Port Edward main beach

The Green Net hosted a renewable-energy awareness event at Port Edward main beach as part of a global movement calling for divestment from fossil fuels. Leaders Chris Viljoen, Joan Gallagher and Helen Dodge participated despite cold, wet weather.

Dec 2023 Completed

E-waste recycling initiative

Helen Dodge photographed and promoted the e-waste collection service run by TeqTronic at Desmond Centre on Marine Drive. The Green Net asked residents to contact them with e-waste disposal information and encouraged diversion of electronics from landfill.

Feb 2024 Ongoing

Bendigo Nature Reserve working groups

Regular collaboration with Bendigo Conservation Group, Rotary Club of Port Shepstone, and Creston College learners to remove alien invasive plants, restore coastal forest, and maintain nature trails in the four-hectare botanical reserve in Southport.

2023–2025 Completed

KZN Biodiversity Calendar — annual series

The Green Net produced and distributed annual illustrated wall calendars pairing an indigenous plant each month with a common Alien Invasive Plant. Includes multilingual names, propagation tips, lunar planting guides, and environmental holidays. Distributed across 20+ stockists from Port Edward to Hillcrest. Proceeds support local conservation.

Mar 2026 Ongoing

EcoBrick bench programme — Tweni & Secret Sands

The Green Net’s EcoBrick programme expanded into a national legacy project in collaboration with Barbara Lawrence-Strydom and Africa I See NPC. Built the fifth EcoBrick bench at Secret Sands Hotel, Glenmore, as part of the In-Visible Women ecHo Initiative. Bench features handprints painted by community members and will include a QR code for tribute messages.

Aug 2026 Planned

In-Visible Women ecHo Bench expansion

National expansion of the EcoBrick bench project. Goal: at least 7 memorial benches and 700 indigenous and fruit trees planted nationwide by the 70th commemoration of the Women’s March on Parliament. Each bench includes a QR code for visitors to read and leave tribute notes.

2026 Planned

River Rangers implementation

Seeking community volunteers and funding to operationalise the River Rangers water-monitoring and river-cleanup systems across the Ugu region. Focus on the Mzimkhulu river system and other polluted waterways.

2026 Planned

2026 KZN Biodiversity Calendar

Fourth edition in production, seeking 12 sponsors/advertisers. Continuing the indigenous plant + AIP education model with new species and updated distribution network.

Ongoing Planned

Reforestation & AIP eradication scaling

Expanding removal of sisal, syringa, lantana, montanoa, and other Category 1B/2 invasive species; replacing with indigenous flora. Currently active in Southport, Sea Park, Louisiana, Munster, and Bendigo. Aims to scale across the entire Ugu region.
