The learning programme.

Training at Indilinde is led by residents, craftspeople, teachers and specialists. Every programme carries an honest status — underway, growing, or planned — and every lesson is something you can take down the hill and use.

Training at Indilinde is led by residents, craftspeople, teachers and specialists. Some of it has started; the rest is being built. Everything carries an honest status.

UnderwayGrowingPlanned

The introductory ecotourism workshops have already run. Next comes specialised training for residents who want to work in tourism, built around the places and subjects guides will actually be asked about.

The aim is simple: local people becoming the most knowledgeable guides this mountain could have.

Local craftspeople will be invited to run practical workshops on the mountain, passing skills from hand to hand. Where appropriate, visitors will be able to join and buy directly from the makers.

The aim is simple: keep traditional skills alive, and make sure they pay.

LeatherworkWoodworkBeadworkGrass workClay workContemporary crafts

The site will demonstrate environmentally responsible food production, combining theory with practical installations that residents can replicate at home.

Every garden on the mountain is a lesson, and every lesson is something you can take down the hill and use.

Permaculture designOrganic gardeningIndigenous & useful plantsSoil conservationCompostingWater-wise growing

Training in rondavel and beehive hut construction, natural building materials and traditional techniques, taught by people who carry that knowledge.

A long-term goal is a building manual documenting traditional construction techniques: a record of indigenous architectural knowledge that might otherwise disappear.

Rondavel constructionBeehive hutsNatural materialsBuilding manualHeritage record

The academy focuses on communication and confidence rather than grammar drills, for local adults, students, teachers, and anyone working towards a future in tourism and hospitality.

The programme is led by Helen Dodge, who lectured at Vista University for eight years and has also taught at a number of schools.

Conversational EnglishEnglish for tourismEnglish for hospitalityEnglish for teachersPublic speakingWriting & communication

Visual art activities for local residents: drawing, painting, mixed media, environmental art and community art projects.

Helen Dodge is also a painter. Her work can be seen at helendodge.co.za.

Indilinde will host specialists for occasional workshops and presentations. The exact programme will evolve as partnerships develop.
