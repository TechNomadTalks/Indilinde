The Green Net NPC is a registered non-profit company (NPC) launched on 29 February 2020 by Helen Dodge and fellow South Coast residents. It promotes and teaches environmentally sustainable practices on the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast, in the Ugu region — through community food gardens, recycling programmes, reforestation, the River Rangers project, and the KZN Biodiversity Calendar. Unlike most NGOs, it operates as a network, not a hierarchy: connecting people and projects rather than directing them.

Donations by direct EFT only. Email us to receive banking details. 100% of your donation goes to the NPC. Section 18A tax receipts available — ask when you donate.

Indilinde works on communal land at kwaNguza with the permission and partnership of the Enhlanhleni community.

Donations by direct EFT via email only. The NPC receives 100% of your donation. Section 18A tax receipts available — ask when you donate.

The Green Net NPC's projects include the River Rangers water programme, community food gardens, KZN Biodiversity Calendar, recycling initiatives, reforestation, Alien Invasive Plant removal, and opposition to offshore oil drilling on the Wild Coast.
