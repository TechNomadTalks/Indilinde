Overview

A mountain that teaches.

Indilinde is a community-built place of learning on a mountain at kwaNguza, behind Umzumbe on the KwaZulu-Natal south coast. It sits at the intersection of nature, culture, education and sustainable tourism — built by The Green Net NPC with Enhlanhleni Development NPO, starting with the land and the people who already live on it.

What makes The Green Net NPC different from most South African NGOs: it is a network, not a hierarchy. It connects people, projects, and markets across the Ugu region, letting initiatives grow from the ground up rather than being imposed from above. No single-issue silos. No donor-driven photo ops. Just compost bins, seedlings, workshops, and families feeding themselves — measured in outcomes, not promises.

The Green Net NPC's projects include the River Rangers water programme, community food gardens, KZN Biodiversity Calendar, recycling initiatives, reforestation, Alien Invasive Plant removal, and opposition to offshore oil drilling on the Wild Coast.
